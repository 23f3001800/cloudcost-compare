Common questions

Why is my S3 bill so much higher than the storage price suggests?

Because storage is rarely the largest line. AWS charges $0.09 per GB to send data out to the internet after the first 100 GB each month. If you store 1 TB but serve 10 TB, egress is roughly 80% of your bill and the $0.023/GB storage rate is almost irrelevant. Put your real numbers into the calculator and look at the Egress column.

Is Cloudflare R2 really free to egress?

Yes — R2 charges $0 for egress with no cap, and that is the entire basis of its pricing model rather than a promotional tier. You still pay $0.015/GB-month for storage plus Class A (write) and Class B (read) operation fees, so R2 is not free. It is free specifically at the bandwidth line, which for most read-heavy workloads is the line that matters.

Which object storage is cheapest overall?

There is no single answer, and any article that gives you one is guessing at your workload. It depends on your egress-to-storage ratio. Store a lot and serve little, and Backblaze B2 at $0.006/GB usually wins. Serve far more than you store, and R2's zero egress wins by a wide margin. Archive-heavy workloads may beat both with S3 Glacier, which this tool does not model.

What is not included in these numbers?

Committed-use discounts, reserved capacity, enterprise agreements, cross-region replication, early-deletion penalties, and per-continent egress rate variation. Wasabi's 90-day minimum retention and Backblaze's 3x free egress allowance are modelled. The methodology page lists every assumption.

Does this tool send my numbers anywhere?

No. It is plain JavaScript running in your browser with no backend to send anything to. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and it will keep working.
