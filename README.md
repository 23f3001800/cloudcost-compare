# CloudCost Compare

Real object storage costs across S3, R2, B2, Wasabi, GCS, Azure and Spaces — egress included.

**Live:** https://23f3001800.github.io/cloudcost-compare/

Static, client-side only. No backend, no accounts, no tracking of your inputs — everything is computed in the browser.

## Pages

- **Calculator** — Compare real monthly object storage costs across AWS S3, Cloudflare R2, Backblaze B2, Wasabi, Google Cloud, Azure and DigitalOcean Spaces. Egress and request fees included. Free, no signup.
- **S3 vs R2** — A direct cost comparison of AWS S3 and Cloudflare R2 at 1 TB, 10 TB and 100 TB of monthly egress, plus the migration caveats nobody mentions.
- **Egress explained** — What egress fees are, why cloud providers charge 100x their cost for bandwidth, which providers have dropped them, and how to estimate yours before the bill arrives.
- **Methodology** — Exactly how CloudCost Compare calculates each provider's monthly cost, which list prices are used, when they were last verified, and the assumptions that could make your real bill differ.
